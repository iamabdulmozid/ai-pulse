An AI-native command centre for sweater sourcing

Premium, calm, precise. Warm near-black surfaces, one UI sans with tabular figures, a mono face for identifiers and timestamps, and a single teal accent reserved for things the AI produced. Status colours mean the same thing on every screen. Dark is the presentation theme; light is the daily one. Everything below is implementable in Tailwind + shadcn/ui with these tokens mapped to the theme.
