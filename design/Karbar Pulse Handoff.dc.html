Assumptions

  • Fresh brand for Karbar; the attached Q Digital codebase was used only for domain patterns (assistant tool steps, KPI provenance, freshness). A faithful recreation of its dashboard, assistant and login sits in Q Digital Baseline for reference.
  • "Today" is Thu 15 Oct 2026, 09:40 Dhaka. Friday is the weekly holiday; what-if overtime days are Fridays.
  • Risk score 0–100 and on-time probability come from one model (v3.2). Status thresholds: On track ≥ 80%, Watch 60–80%, At risk 30–60% or slip 1–4 days, Critical < 30% or slip ≥ 5 days, Late = past ex-factory and not shipped, No update = no factory report by 09:30.
  • Value at risk = FOB of POs with on-time probability under 60%. Air-freight exposure assumes a 40% air split at USD 4.10/kg and 0.69 kg/pc average for 7GG lambswool.
  • Hero PO slip arithmetic: yarn in-house +8 days, linking shortfall +4, knitting recovery −3 = 9 days late. Each extra linking machine recovers ~1 day; each Friday overtime day ~1.5 days (6 machines + 2 Fridays → on time).
  • Factory names are fictional. 22 factories, 412 open POs, 1,912,400 pcs, USD 18.6M. The demo dataset shows 20 representative POs; counts in filters are the full book.
  • Settings is deferred (LATER). Phone (390 px) is covered for Overview, PO detail, Alerts and Assistant via responsive rules in the same prototype.

Build notes

  • Tokens: copy the [data-theme] variable blocks from the design system into globals.css; map them in tailwind.config.ts under colors (surface, surface2, surface3, border, hair, text, text2, muted, faint, accent, ai, status.*, stage.*). Set font-variant-numeric on body.
  • shadcn: Card, Badge (status variants), Tooltip (source tooltip), Dialog + Command (⌘K), Sheet (assistant slide-over), Slider (what-if), Tabs, Table (TanStack), Toast (alerts).
  • Recharts: KPI sparkline = LineChart 64×22 no axes; outlook = stacked BarChart; curves = ComposedChart (Area band + Lines + ReferenceLines); factory output = small LineCharts; on-time trend = LineChart with Area band. Heatmap and T&A timeline are custom CSS grid components.
  • Streaming: SSE events step (id, label, status, ms), delta (text), artifact (table | chart spec), sources, followups, done, error. Render steps before text; collapse steps after done.
  • Freshness: every API payload carries as_of and reports_today: {received, expected}; the header pill, KPI tooltips and chat sources all read the same values.
